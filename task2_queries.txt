Task 2 - Prolog Family Queries
================================

1. Shows the names of all females:
   ?- female(X).
   Result: X = sara ; X = nora.

2. Shows whether there is any male:
   ?- male(_).
   Result: true.

3. Shows the grandparent of Faisal:
   ?- parent(X, Y), parent(Y, faisal).
   Result: X = ali, Y = muhammad.

4. Shows all daughters of Ahmad:
   ?- parent(ahmad, X), female(X).
   Result: X = sara ; X = nora.

5. Shows whether Ahmad and Muhammad have a common parent:
   ?- parent(X, ahmad), parent(X, muhammad).
   Result: X = ali. (true - ali is the common parent)

6. Shows whether Khan is the brother of Nora:
   ?- male(khan), parent(P, khan), parent(P, nora).
   Result: P = ahmad. (true - khan is the brother of nora)
